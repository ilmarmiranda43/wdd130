Featured Trips

Half-Day Family Float

Perfect for beginners and families with kids ages 6 and up. Gentle Class I–II rapids along a calm, scenic section of the river.

  • Duration: 3–4 hours
  • Difficulty: Easy
  • Includes: Professional guide, safety gear, snacks

Full-Day Canyon Run

Our most popular trip! Spend a full day exploring canyons, enjoying powerful rapids, and stopping for a riverside lunch.

  • Duration: 7–8 hours
  • Difficulty: Moderate (Class II–III)
  • Includes: Lunch, drinks, photos, safety gear

Los Dientes del Diablo

For experienced rafters looking for a challenge. Intense rapids, narrow channels, and powerful waves make this a true adventure.

  • Duration: 5–6 hours
  • Difficulty: Advanced (Class IV)
  • Includes: Expert guide team, safety briefing, gear

Big Mallard Challenge

A technical run with strong drops and tight lines. Ideal for adventurous guests ready to take the next step.

  • Duration: Half-day
  • Difficulty: Intermediate–Advanced (Class III–IV)
  • Includes: Guide, gear, rapid-by-rapid coaching
